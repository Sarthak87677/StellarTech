Our mission

Engineering practice, not a hobby club.

We exist so students can carry an idea all the way through: research it, design it, build it, test it in the field, document what actually happened, and publish honest results — including the ones that did not work.

Every project runs on the discipline a professional lab would use. Work is logged, reviewed by peers, and reflected on. Claims are backed by evidence or labelled as untested.

Flagship programme

Ultimate Rakhandar

A heritage-preservation robotics programme. A ground rover carries a sensor package around a structure and builds a documented record of its condition — so change over time becomes measurable instead of remembered.

Rakhandar is an active research programme. Site details, scan data and internal reports are restricted to authorised members.

Read the project overview

  • PrototypeCrack and surface-defect detection from stereo depth vision
  • PrototypeThermal array survey for moisture and sub-surface indicators
  • PlannedBaseline record on first visit, comparison on every visit after
  • Future moduleAerial pass — only where ground coverage is incomplete, and only with approval

How membership works

A route in, and a route forward.

Step 01 — Apply

Send an application

Tell us what you want to work on and what you already know. No prior experience required — curiosity and follow-through matter more.

Step 02 — Review

Founder review

Every application is read by a person. Approved applicants receive their own private account for the member workspace.

Step 03 — Contribute

Join a project team

Pick up real tasks, log your research and build hours, upload evidence of your work and write short reflections on what you learned.

Step 04 — Lead

Grow into responsibility

Consistent contributors take on organising-committee roles, mentor newer members and help set project direction.
